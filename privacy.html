Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026

Sometimes I email a business once to offer a free mock-up of a website or booking page. This page says what I keep about you when I do that, why I keep it, for how long, and how to make me stop.

Who I am

I'm Pol Danyael Villorente, a web developer working from the Philippines. I decide what gets kept, and I handle it myself.

To reach me about anything on this page, email pllxrgn@gmail.com.

Who this covers

Businesses in the Philippines and the United States that I have emailed, or may email, about a website or booking page. If the address I wrote to has your name in it, this covers you as a person too.

What I keep

  • The public email address I wrote to.
  • A contact name, only if the business shows it on its own public page.
  • The business name and its city.
  • Notes on the public pages that led me to write, for example that booking is by phone only, with each page's web address and the date I read it. These notes can include a phone number or business address the business publishes.
  • The date I sent the email, and whether you replied or asked me to stop.
  • The email itself and any replies, in my Gmail.

I don't keep anything else about you.

Where it comes from

Only from pages the business publishes itself, or that list it in public:

  • its own website;
  • its own Google Maps or Facebook page;
  • public business directories and listings;
  • public ad libraries, such as Meta's Ad Library;
  • public posts asking for a web developer.

Now and then a business owner I already know suggests I get in touch with you.

I read pages one at a time. I don't buy lists, I don't run bulk scrapers, and if a site blocks me, I leave it.

Why I use it

To send you one email offering a free mock-up of a website or booking page for your business. I don't send follow-ups. If you reply, I answer you.

If you say yes, I build the mock-up from your public pages. It sits on an unlisted link that search engines are told not to show, and I take it down the day you ask.

The Philippine Data Privacy Act (Republic Act 10173) covers details that identify a person, such as an email address with your name in it. Where it applies, the basis I rely on is legitimate interest under Section 12(f). My interest is offering a service that fits your business. I keep it small: one email to the address you publish, no follow-ups, and I stop when you ask.

No tracking

My emails carry no open-tracking pixel and no link made for you alone. I don't add you to a newsletter or a mailing list.

Some emails I sent before 19 November 2026 linked my demo site with a tag for the whole batch, such as "wave8". The demo site counts visits per batch, not per person. The first emails I send now link only this portfolio, which runs no analytics.

Where it is kept, and who sees it

In text files on my own computer, and in my Gmail account. Google runs Gmail, so those emails sit on Google's servers, which can be outside the Philippines.

I use an AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, to help me read public pages, write my notes and draft emails, so those details pass through Anthropic's service while I work.

I don't sell your details or give them to anyone else, and I use them only for the email described above.

How long I keep it

Twelve months after our last contact. Then I delete it from my notes and my mailbox.

If you ask me to stop, I keep only your email address and the date you asked, on a do-not-contact list, so I never email you again.

Your choices and rights

To stop my emails, reply "stop", "no thanks" or anything like it. I put you on the do-not-contact list the same day I read your reply. The US CAN-SPAM Act gives senders 10 business days to honor an opt-out (FTC compliance guide). I don't wait that long.

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be told whether and how your details are used;
  • see the details I hold about you and where I got them;
  • object to their use, including for direct marketing;
  • have wrong details corrected;
  • have them blocked, removed or deleted;
  • get a copy in a common electronic format;
  • claim damages if misuse of your details harms you.

You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

To use any of these rights, email me at pllxrgn@gmail.com. I'll reply as soon as I can, and within 15 days.

This website

This portfolio runs no analytics, and its pages set no cookies. GitHub hosts it on GitHub Pages, and it loads fonts from Google Fonts and one script from jsDelivr, so those services see your IP address when a page loads, as any web server does. If you use the contact form on the portfolio, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes your name, email and message on to my email.

Changes to this notice

When I change this notice, I change the date at the top of this page.
